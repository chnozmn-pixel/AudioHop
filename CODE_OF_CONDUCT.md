# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make taking part in
AudioHop a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps this project:

- Being kind and patient with people, including beginners.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Owning our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Caring about what is best for the community, not only for ourselves.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces - issues, pull requests,
discussions and commit messages - and also when someone is representing the
project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/chnozmn-pixel/AudioHop/security/advisories/new),
which only the maintainer can read, or by contacting the maintainer through
their GitHub profile at https://github.com/chnozmn-pixel.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The
maintainer respects the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the project's
leadership.

### What happens next

Depending on how serious the behaviour is, the response may be a private
warning, a temporary ban from interacting with the project, or a permanent ban.
Where the response is a warning, it will say what was wrong and what is
expected instead.
